Does the pressure index separate hard situations from ordinary ones?

High-pressure deliveries score at the same pace as ordinary ones, but wickets fall about 50% faster and the batting side wins far less often. Over half of them come before the death overs.

Sensitivity: how stable are the lists?

Each row changes one assumption and counts how many of the original ten names stay in the top ten. The baseline is a 70th-percentile cutoff, a 5-run batter dismissal penalty, a 6-run bowler wicket bonus and a 60-delivery prior.

Method

Pressure index

Each delivery is scored from the match state just before it is bowled, from 0 (low) to 100 (high). The cutoff is the 70th percentile of all deliveries.

Second innings (chase)
P = 100 x [ 0.55 x clip((RRR - 8.0) / 5, 0, 1)
          + 0.30 x clip(wickets_lost / 8, 0, 1)
          + 0.15 x innings_progress ]

First innings
P = 100 x [ 0.48 x clip(wickets_lost / 8, 0, 1)
          + 0.32 x clip((8.5 - CRR) / 4, 0, 1)
          + 0.20 x innings_progress ]

RRR is the required run rate and CRR the current run rate, both before the delivery. Phases: powerplay overs 1–6, middle 7–15, death 16–20.

Player ratings
BatterValue = batter_runs - expected_batter_runs - 5 x credited_dismissal
BowlerValue = expected_bowler_runs - bowler_charged_runs + 6 x bowler_credited_wicket

ReliabilityScore = HPValue x HPBalls/(HPBalls+60) + OverallValue x 60/(HPBalls+60)
PressureDelta    = HPValue - OverallValue
CLUTCH Score     = 0.65 x ReliabilityScore + 0.35 x PressureDelta

Expected runs come from the format, innings and phase average. Byes and leg-byes are not charged to the bowler. Run-outs and retired or obstructing dismissals are not credited to batters or bowlers. The 60-delivery prior pulls small samples toward the player's overall baseline.

Eligibility filters
  • Batters: at least 80 high-pressure balls, 8 pressure matches and 150 total batting deliveries (154 eligible).
  • Bowlers: at least 100 high-pressure balls, 8 pressure matches and 150 total bowling deliveries (161 eligible).
Limitations
  • Pressure is an operational definition, not a measure of a player's state of mind.
  • The 8.0 RRR and 8.5 CRR reference points are fixed benchmarks, not venue-specific par scores.
  • The cutoff is distribution-based (top 30%); a different cutoff changes the rankings.
  • Values are additive approximations. They do not model win probability, fielding, partnerships, pitch, boundary size or opposition strength.
  • The 5-run and 6-run weights are model choices, not scoring rules.
  • Several leading players have small pressure samples, and scores around 10th place are tightly packed.
  • Only the supplied competitions and dates are covered, and no outside information is used.
Data lineage

Scores come from ball_by_ball.csv, matches.csv and competitions.csv. playing_eleven.csv, competition_team_player_mapping.csv and teams.csv are used for cross-checks and team context: all 388 pressure player-matches of the 20 ranked players appear in the playing XI file, and all 348 squad-listing team pairs agree with teams.csv. 26 super-over deliveries are retained in the baselines; excluding them swaps the 10th and 11th bowlers.

The ranking is a statistical shortlist computed only from the supplied data pack. Methodology references: Mallawa Arachchi, Manage & Scariano (2024); Irvine & Kennedy (2017); Hassan & Rafique (2022).
